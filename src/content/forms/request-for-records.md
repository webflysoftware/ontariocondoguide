## What this form is for

Under the **Condominium Act, 1998** and **Ontario Regulation 48/01**, owners, purchasers, and mortgagees (or their authorized agents) must use the mandatory **Request for Records** form to ask a condominium corporation for its records. Using the exact prescribed form is required — a corporation can decline a request that does not use it.

## When to use it

- You want to review or obtain copies of **core records** such as the declaration, by-laws and rules, budget, financial statements, auditor's report, reserve fund plan, meeting minutes, or the record of owners and mortgagees.
- You are requesting **non-core records** (subject to reasonable fees).
- You are a purchaser or mortgagee entitled to review records before or during a transaction.

## What happens next

The corporation has **30 days** to respond using the mandatory **Board's Response to Request for Records** form. It must state, for each record, whether access or a copy will be provided, any applicable fee, and — if it refuses — the provision of the Act that allows the refusal.

## What you'll need

- The full legal name of the condominium corporation.
- Your unit or common-interest identification (from your deed or status certificate).
- Your mailing address in Ontario.

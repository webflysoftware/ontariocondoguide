## How to use

Enter your **target AGM date** and optional **fiscal year end**. The calculator shows suggested deadlines working backwards — when to start planning, send preliminary notice, send meeting notice, and close proxy collection.

Adjust the notice periods if your declaration or bylaws require longer timelines than the common defaults.

## What this calculator covers

Ontario condo AGMs involve layered deadlines:

- **Statutory AGM window** — generally within six months after fiscal year-end
- **Preliminary notice of meeting** — often required before the formal notice package
- **Meeting notice** — minimum period before the AGM (commonly 15 days under the Condominium Act, 1998)
- **Proxy cut-off** — set by the corporation in the notice package
- **Internal planning** — audited financials, nominations, and quorum outreach

This tool combines those into one timeline. Pair it with the [AGM readiness check](/tools/agm-readiness-check) for a task checklist and the [quorum calculator](/tools/quorum-calculator) for participation math.

## Important disclaimer

Default values are educational starting points only. Your corporation's **declaration, bylaws, and meeting type** control. Requisitioned meetings, turnover meetings, and contested elections may need different lead times. Confirm with legal counsel before sending notice.

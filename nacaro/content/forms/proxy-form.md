## What this form is for

Under **section 13 of Ontario Regulation 48/01**, an instrument appointing a proxy for a meeting of owners must use the mandatory **Proxy Form**. It lets an owner or mortgagee (the *proxy giver*) authorize another person (the *proxy*) to attend and vote on their behalf.

## When to use it

- You cannot attend an owners' meeting (such as the AGM) in person and want your vote counted.
- You want to help the meeting **reach quorum** without granting voting authority.
- You want to appoint someone to **vote on specific matters** or to **elect directors** on your behalf.

## Choosing the scope of authority

The form requires you to choose **one** of three authority levels:

- **Quorum only** — counts you toward quorum but casts no votes.
- **Routine procedure only** — the proxy may vote on routine motions only.
- **Full authority** — the proxy may nominate candidates and vote on any matter, subject to any instructions you provide.

## What you'll need

- The corporation name and the meeting date.
- Your name and unit identification.
- The name of the person you are appointing (and, optionally, a backup).

Fill it out below with guidance, then download a completed PDF to print and sign. A proxy is only valid when signed by the proxy giver and delivered before the meeting deadline.

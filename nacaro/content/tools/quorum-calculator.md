## How to use this tool

Enter your association's total voting units, required quorum percentage, and current participation counts. The calculator shows whether quorum appears met and how many additional units are needed.

## Before you start

Confirm in your **declaration, CC&Rs, or bylaws**:

- Total number of voting units or voting interests
- Quorum percentage or fixed number required
- Whether proxies and advance votes count toward quorum

This tool provides planning math only. The chair must still confirm quorum using your official registration and proxy records at the meeting.

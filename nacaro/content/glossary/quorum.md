## In plain language

If not enough owners participate — in person, by proxy, or by another permitted method — the meeting may not be able to elect directors or approve major votes.

## Key points

| Topic | Typical rule |
|-------|--------------|
| Where defined | CC&Rs, bylaws, and state law |
| Common threshold | 10%–50% of units or voting interests |
| Proxies | Usually count if valid |
| Failed quorum | Meeting may adjourn or reschedule |

## Good minutes include

- Meeting date, time, and type
- Directors or owners present
- Quorum confirmation for membership meetings
- Motions, seconds, and vote results
- Adjournment time

Minutes are not a transcript of every comment. They record **actions taken**.

Owners may have statutory rights to inspect certain minutes. Confirm your state's records access rules.

## Common documents

| Document | Typical purpose |
|----------|-----------------|
| Declaration / CC&Rs | Creates restrictions, assessments, and basic governance |
| Bylaws | Meeting procedures, board structure, elections |
| Articles of incorporation | Corporate formation |
| Rules and regulations | Day-to-day conduct and use restrictions |

Always read **your** documents — form titles and content vary by state and developer.

## In plain language

Some associations assign one vote per unit. Others allocate votes by percentage interest or lot type. Quorum and election math depend on how voting interests are defined in your documents.

## Where to confirm

- Declaration or CC&Rs schedule of voting interests
- Bylaws election provisions
- State HOA or condo statute

Use the [Quorum Calculator](/tools/quorum-calculator) after confirming your total voting units or interests.

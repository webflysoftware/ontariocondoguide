## What bylaws usually cover

- Annual and special meeting procedures
- Notice periods
- Quorum for membership and board meetings
- Director terms and nomination rules
- Officer roles

Bylaws must be read together with the declaration and state law. If they conflict, state law or the declaration may control depending on jurisdiction.
